Research

Technical investigations related to financial-impacting cyber risk in SMEs.

Current research focuses on the Cyveris Risk Index (CRI), including:

  • Adaptation of prudential risk estimation
  • Integration with existing stochastic and exposure-driven models
  • Application of Poisson-based event probability frameworks
  • Alignment with operational infrastructure exposure indicators

Ongoing work aims to contextualize established probabilistic risk modeling approaches within financially-constrained digital infrastructures.
